## Standard Version（推荐版本，约 400 词）

```
Subject: Pre-submission inquiry — VSI: CAV-Safety-TRA — Operational Governance in L4 AV Deployment

Dear Dr. Yang, Dr. Lu, and the Guest Editor Team,

We are writing to inquire about a manuscript intended for the
Transportation Research Part A special issue "Connected and Automated
Vehicle Governance: Evidence, Ethics, and Policy Innovation"
(VSI: CAV-Safety-TRA). The manuscript is co-authored by Yuxin Zhang
(Jilin University) and Pan Li (Tongji University, formerly with
Waymo's Server-Side Perception and Oracle Perception teams). Pan Li's
production experience at Waymo provides a distinctive empirical anchor
for this work.

The paper argues that while vehicle type approval and safety event
data interaction are well-codified internationally, "operational
governance" — who decides what to do when an L4 vehicle fails in
service, and how regulators verify and respond — remains a global
gap. The Wuhan Robotaxi mass shutdown of March 2026 (in which
approximately one hundred Robotaxis stalled simultaneously on a
highway, stranding passengers for two hours) and the Markey U.S.
Senate investigation of the same month (in which seven leading AV
companies refused to disclose remote-intervention frequencies)
exposed this gap from opposite directions. California DMV Article
3.7 Express Terms 2026 is the first regulation to systematically
address it.

Our four contributions are: (1) a systematic comparison of
operational governance frameworks across the U.S., China, Germany,
and international standards bodies; (2) policy-relevant analysis of
three high-impact case studies (Wuhan 2026, Markey 2026, Cruise SF
2023); (3) ROAM (Remote Operations and Anomaly Management) — an
open-source reference architecture (github.com/AutoZYX-Labs/ROAM,
v1.2; aligned with seventeen national and international standards)
comprising ten operating modes, twenty-nine anomaly sub-scenarios,
a three-layer decision architecture, a seven-dimensional
responsibility matrix newly incorporating regulatory feedback, and
sixteen quantitative KPIs; and (4) empirical calibration using
Waymo deployment metrics, including the characterization of an
"irreducible 4-5%" of decisions requiring human Layer-3
intervention.

The paper directly addresses three themes in the special issue
Call for Papers: "AV safety regulation and deployment decisions",
"ethical frameworks and accountability for automated decision-
making", and "comparative studies of AV safety policy across
cultural, institutional, and political contexts". We close with
five specific recommendations for Chinese regulators, three for
international standardization, and a compliance design checklist
for fleet operators.

A 300-word abstract is attached. Could you kindly indicate whether
the paper falls within the special issue scope? A detailed outline
(approximately 5,000 words across eight sections, with twelve
figures and tables planned and a 60–80 reference target) is
available upon request. We aim to submit the complete manuscript
well before the December 31, 2026 deadline.

Thank you for organizing this timely special issue. We look forward
to your response.

Best regards,

Yuxin Zhang, on behalf of co-authors

---
Yuxin Zhang, PhD
Associate Professor, College of Automotive Engineering
Director, AD Safety Joint Lab
Jilin University
yuxinzhang@jlu.edu.cn
```

## 邮件设计说明（给共同作者评审用）

### Standard Version 选择理由

Pre-submission inquiry 的行业惯例长度是 250-400 词。本邮件采用偏长版本（~400 词）是有意为之：

- TR-A 是政策类期刊，GE 会偏向看到"问题陈述 + 方法 + 贡献"完整三段式
- 武汉事件 + Markey 投资是英文学术圈相对陌生的中国 / 美国新闻，简短描述帮助 GE 快速 grasp
- 4 条 contributions 的具体性是与其他 inquiries 的差异化关键，不能压缩
- 三个 CfP 主题精准引用是显示我们认真读过 CfP 的最强信号

### 关键策略

1. 第一段开场 hook —— 明确 Pan Li 的 Waymo 经验作为"distinctive empirical anchor"——这是与其他 inquiries 最大的差异化，必须前置

2. 第二段问题陈述 —— 用三个具体事件（Wuhan / Markey / DMV Article 3.7）作为锚点，避免抽象论证

3. 第三段方法 + 框架 —— 4 条 contributions 编号清晰，让 GE 能快速判断哪条对应他们的 CfP 主题

4. 第四段精准对应 CfP 主题 —— 直接引用 3 条 CfP 原文，让 GE 看到我们做过功课

5. 第五段提供路径 —— abstract 已附 + outline 待索取，给 GE 渐进信息层级

6. 签名包含 ROAM 链接 —— 让 GE 可以 "verify trust" — 开源仓库 + 网站是可点击验证的可信度信号

### 何时使用 Concise Version

- Standard 版本发出后 10-14 天无回复，做 polite follow-up
- 如果 GE 在第一封邮件回复了简短问题（"Can you summarize in one paragraph?"）
- 如果直接发到 TR-A 编辑系统的 pre-submission portal（通常字数限制更严）

### 不要做的事

- 不要在邮件中提及"我们参与 CSAE 立项"——这会让 GE 怀疑利益冲突
- 不要在邮件中提及英国访问学者 / 张玉新的其他身份——保持纯研究者口径
- 不要列举太多 ROAM 开源项目数据（CFR：567 incidents、17 standards 等放 abstract 即可）
- 不要直接 attach 完整 outline（5000 词太长，让 GE 索取以示尊重）
- 不要使用 "I would be honored if you could..."、"I humbly request..." 等卑微语气

### 时机建议

> 注：之前张老师指示不再做月级时间规划。本节仅给"信号性"建议，不约束你的实际节奏。

发送 inquiry 后建议给 GE 留 7-14 天回复时间（学术邮件的礼貌窗口）。如果想加速，可以同时联系：

- TR-A 主编（直接联系 EiC，但通常 special issue 由 GE 全权负责，不绕过 GE）
- 通过李盼联系明尼苏达 Stern（如李盼有 Waymo 时期 Stern 课题组合作关系）
